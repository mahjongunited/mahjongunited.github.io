Our biggest afternoon of the year. Four rounds of Hong Kong–style play, scored on a points ladder, with prizes for the top three tables and a wooden spoon for the most adventurous discard.

**What to bring:** nothing but yourself. Sets, scoring sheets and tea are provided.

**Entry:** $20 at the door, $15 if you reserve ahead. Spectators welcome.
