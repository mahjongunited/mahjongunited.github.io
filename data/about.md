Teacher, host, and founder of Mahjong United. Jennie grew up playing at her grandmother's table and has been teaching the game for over a decade — in living rooms, community halls, and now a studio of her own.

Mahjong United runs tournaments and socials, weekly open classes, and private lessons by reservation.
